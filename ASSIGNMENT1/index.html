About ABES

At ABES Engineering College, we ignite the flames of curiosity, knowledge, and innovation. Established over two decades ago, our institution has evolved into a dynamic center of learning nestled in the heart of Ghaziabad. Beyond engineering, our academic spectrum encompasses M.Tech, MCA, and MBA programs designed to cater to the multifaceted aspirations of our students.
